// Got Questions

FAQ

Drop 001 is a pre-order drop. All orders ship within 6–8 weeks of the pre-order window closing. You'll get an email notification with tracking info when your package ships.

Since Drop 001 is a pre-order, all sales are final. However, if we make an error on your order (wrong item, wrong size), we'll make it right. Hit us up at contact@nvll.com within 7 days of receiving your order.

Drop 001 runs XS through XXL. Our tee is designed oversized and boxy — if you prefer a fitted look, size down. If you're going for that streetwear drip, go true to size or size up.

NVLL ships worldwide. International shipping rates vary depending on your location and will be calculated at Stripe checkout. Allow additional time for international deliveries — typically 2–4 weeks on top of production time.

This is just the beginning. NVLL drops in limited quantities — once a drop is gone, it's gone. Follow us on social and stay on the email list to be the first to know when Drop 002 goes live.
